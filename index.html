How to play

  1. The wheel speed is live from Squeekchain — it moves when Squeektoshi runs and stops when he naps.
  2. Press CATCH while the golden needle is inside the green target zone.
  3. Hits near the middle of the zone score more. Streaks add bonus points. Each round gives you a limited number of tries before the timer runs out.
  4. Every 150 points you level up and the target zone gets narrower.
  5. Tip: press the Space bar (or Enter) to CATCH — no mouse needed.
  6. Need a break? Press P or the Pause button to freeze the round timer and CATCH — press it again to resume.
  7. Pick a difficulty: Easy (wide 50 RPM zone, 12 tries, 90 seconds), Normal (40 RPM zone, 10 tries, 90 seconds), or Hard (narrow 30 RPM zone, 8 tries, 75 seconds). Press 1, 2, or 3 to switch — it starts a fresh round and your choice is remembered.
